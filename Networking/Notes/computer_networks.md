# Computer Networks

## What is a Computer Network?

A computer network is a group of two or more devices connected together to share information and resources.

## Why Do We Use Networks?

- Share files
- Access the Internet
- Share printers
- Communicate between devices
- Share data and resources

## Types of Networks

### LAN (Local Area Network)

- Covers a small area
- Examples:
  - Home Wi-Fi
  - School computer lab
  - Office network

### MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

- Covers a city
- Example:
  - University campuses connected across a city

### WAN (Wide Area Network)

- Covers large geographic areas
- Example:
  - The Internet

## Quick Comparison

| Network | Coverage |
|---------|----------|
| LAN | Home, Office, Building |
| MAN | City |
| WAN | Country or World |

## Key Points

- LAN = Small Area
- MAN = City
- WAN = Large Area
- The Internet is a WAN.
